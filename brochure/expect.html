What to expect

Plain notes so a first visit is not a mystery. These are practical, not a treatment plan.

Before

Text or call with the town, and whether you want 30 or 60 minutes. Mobile is the hour only. If there is a travel fee from Burford, it is named before she comes. Say if you want lighter work, Therapeutic Touch, or more fascia and pressure. Say what you do not want.

Arriving

Come as you are. You do not need a story about why you booked. A private-location visit is at the arranged place. A mobile visit needs enough floor for a table and a quiet room.

During

The session may move between relaxation massage, deep tissue, fascia release, intuitive bodywork, Therapeutic Touch, energy work, and quiet restorative touch. Pressure stays inside your comfort and pain tolerance. You can ask for less. You can ask her to stop.

After

The point of the closer is the line on the flyer: slow down, reconnect with your body, let your system settle back into sync. Drink water if you want. This is not medical aftercare.
