Sessions

Two lengths. The shorter one stays at a private location. The hour can come to you.

30 minutes — $50

Private location only. Useful when the work is Therapeutic Touch, or a focused area, rather than a full-body hour. This length is not offered as a mobile visit.

60 minutes — $100

Mobile or private location. This is the session for fascia release, relaxation massage, and a mix of touch and quieter work. Mobile appointments need this minimum.

A travel fee may apply based on distance from Burford. Serving Brantford, Woodstock, Cambridge, and Kitchener.

What a session may include

Each session is unique. It may include relaxation massage, deep tissue techniques, fascia release, intuitive bodywork, Therapeutic Touch, energy work, and quiet restorative touch. Pressure is adjusted within your comfort level and pain tolerance. You can say lighter or stop at any point.

The massage training was under the guidance of a Registered Massage Therapist. Sol Sync is not an RMT practice, and a session is not a medical treatment.
