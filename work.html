The work

Energy work, said plainly.

This page is a slot for how Ilona actually describes the practice. The sample keeps the claims small on purpose.

What happens

A conversation first. Then a session at an agreed pace, in person or at a distance. You can stop or change course at any point.

What it is not

Not medicine, not psychotherapy, not a promise that a feeling will be gone by the end of the hour.

Who it is for

People who already want this kind of work and can say what they hope to leave with. Final fit is a conversation, not a form.
