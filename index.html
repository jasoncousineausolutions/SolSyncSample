Sol Sync · layout samples

Five site structures, same practice.

These are layouts, not color themes. Each one has room for sessions, reviews, and a later esoteric-study room. Review quotes are empty slots, not invented clients.

01

Brochure

Classic multi-page site. Top nav. Home is only a doorway. Sessions, about, reviews, study, and visit are separate pages. The usual five-or-six page practice site.

02

Long service scroll

One home that walks the offerings the way a spa menu site does, with prices in each block and reserve links. Reviews and study are their own pages, linked from a sticky bar.

03

Fixed rail

Navigation stays in a left column. The page beside it changes. Useful if she wants the study room to feel like a section of the same studio, not a different website.

04

One page per offering

Home is a directory. Fascia, energy work, and relaxation each have a page with who it is for, what happens, length, price, and town. Reviews and study sit beside those, not inside them.

05

Split visit

Half the screen is questions, half is the request. Booking stays in view. Reviews and the study room are linked, not stacked under the flyer text.
